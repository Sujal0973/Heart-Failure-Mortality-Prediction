Do's & Don'ts — Reduce Heart Failure Risk

This page offers general suggestions. It is not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional.

Very Low risk — what to keep doing

  • Maintain medication adherence, healthy diet, and activity.
  • Keep routine follow-ups and monitoring.

Low risk — small improvements

  • Reduce sodium intake; monitor weight for sudden gains.
  • Increase light-moderate activity after physician approval.

Moderate risk — discuss with clinician

  • Schedule a review to check medications, kidney/electrolyte tests.
  • Keep a symptom diary (breathlessness, swelling, fatigue).

High risk — prompt review advised

  • Contact your healthcare provider for urgent review.
  • Watch for rapid weight gain (>2 kg in 2 days), worsening breathlessness, chest pain — seek care.

Very High risk — seek urgent attention

  • Seek emergency care for severe symptoms.
  • Bring medication list and recent results to your provider.
