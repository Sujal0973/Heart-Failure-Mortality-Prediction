CardioPulse AI Decision Support

A clinically calibrated artificial intelligence platform estimating heart failure mortality risk using 12 routine diagnostic parameters.

🫀
Tuned XGBoost Engine
Imbalance-weighted gradient boosting achieving 83.3% test accuracy and 0.879 ROC-AUC on patient cohorts.
🧪
12 Clinical Markers
Synthesizes ejection fraction, renal clearance (creatinine), electrolytes, CPK, and comorbid histories in real-time.
📈
5-Tier Stratification
Maps continuous probabilities into actionable clinical bands: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, and Very High.
