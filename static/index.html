Patient Demographics & Observation Window
Baseline patient baseline status
Patient Age ? Chronological patient age in years. Advanced age (>70) is an established independent cardiovascular mortality factor. 60 yrs
Biological Sex ? Biological sex assigned at birth; correlates with cardiovascular disease presentation trajectories.
Follow-up Monitoring Window (days) ? Days elapsed during patient observation. Mortality vulnerability is heavily concentrated in the acute initial post-event window (<30 days). 100 days
Cardiac Function & Hemodynamics
Left ventricular performance & arterial pressure
Ejection Fraction (%) ? Percentage of blood volume pumped by the left ventricle per beat. Normal: 50–70%. Below 40% denotes Heart Failure with Reduced Ejection Fraction (HFrEF). 50%
Hypertension History ? Chronic arterial high blood pressure; chronic afterload strain accelerates left ventricular remodeling and cardiac fibrosis.
Biochemistry & Renal Clearance Profile
Kidney filtration, electrolytes & enzyme markers
Serum Creatinine (mg/dL) ? Clinical marker of glomerular filtration rate. Normal: 0.7–1.3 mg/dL. Elevated levels (>1.3) indicate cardiorenal syndrome. 1.0 mg/dL
Serum Sodium (mEq/L) ? Electrolyte balance marker. Normal: 135–145 mEq/L. Hyponatremia (<135) signifies potent neurohormonal hyperactivity and poor prognosis. 137 mEq/L
CPK Enzyme (mcg/L) ? Creatinine phosphokinase; intracellular enzyme released upon myocardial or musculoskeletal cellular injury (normal: 10–120 mcg/L). 200 mcg/L
Platelet Count (/mL) ? Platelets involved in hemostasis and inflammatory signaling. Normal range: 150,000 to 450,000 platelets per mL. 263k
Comorbidities & Behavioral Risk Exposures
Secondary systemic diseases and habits
Diabetes Mellitus ? History of diabetes mellitus; impairs microvascular perfusion and accelerates metabolic myocardial stress.
Anaemia ? Reduced hemoglobin count, demanding higher resting cardiac output to meet systemic metabolic demands.
Active Smoking Habit ? Active or recent tobacco smoking history; triggers endothelial damage and oxidative myocardial stress.
